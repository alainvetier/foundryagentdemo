## Recommendations by situation

- **Cat sleeps on your laptop keyboard** → Keyboard Warmer Nap Pad (WW-002). Place it next to the laptop.
- **Indoor cat with too much energy** → Laser Pointer Pro 9000 (WW-001) + Kubernetes Scratching Pod (WW-005). Always finish laser play with a physical toy (e.g. WW-007) so the cat can "catch" something.
- **Cat doesn't drink enough** → Zero-Downtime Water Fountain (WW-009). Moving water encourages drinking.
- **Picky eater / whisker fatigue** → Stack Overflow Food Bowl (WW-010).
- **Budget gift under $10** → Rubber Duck Debugger (WW-003), Git Commit Treats (WW-006), Merge Conflict Mouse (WW-007).
- **Senior cats (8+ years)** → Cloud Nine Cat Bed (WW-008) and Keyboard Warmer Nap Pad (WW-002) for warm joints. Kibble WW-011 is for adults 1-7 years; ask your vet about senior food.

## Safety notes

- Never point the laser at eyes. The Laser Pointer Pro 9000 is Class 1 (eye-safe), but still.
- Remove the catnip pouch from WW-003 if your cat becomes over-excited.
- The heated pad WW-002 must not be covered by blankets.
