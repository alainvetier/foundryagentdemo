# Cat Care Basics (Whisker Works Knowledge Base)

General guidance only. For symptoms, injuries or medication, always consult a licensed veterinarian.

## Hydration
- An adult cat needs roughly **40-60 ml of water per kg of body weight per day** (a 4 kg cat ≈ 160-240 ml), including water from wet food.
- Cats prefer water placed **away from their food bowl**. Fountains help many cats drink more.

## Feeding
- Most adult cats do well with **2-3 measured meals per day** rather than free feeding.
- Treats should be **no more than 10% of daily calories**. Example: a 4 kg indoor cat needs about 200 kcal/day, so max ~20 kcal of treats (10 Git Commit Treats).
- Never feed onions, garlic, chocolate, grapes/raisins, alcohol or xylitol.

## Enrichment
- Indoor cats need **2-3 play sessions of 10-15 minutes** daily.
- Follow the hunting sequence: stalk → chase → pounce → "catch" → eat a small treat → groom → sleep.
- Vertical space (towers, shelves) reduces stress in multi-cat homes.

## Sleep
- Adult cats sleep **12-16 hours** per day; kittens and seniors even more.
- Warm, elevated, enclosed spots are favourites (hence: laptops and boxes).

## Grooming
- Short-haired cats: brush weekly. Long-haired cats: brush daily.
- Trim claws every 2-3 weeks and provide scratching posts with sisal rope.

## Toxic plants
- **Lilies are extremely toxic to cats** (even pollen). Also avoid pothos, sago palm and dieffenbachia.

## When to call the vet immediately
- Not eating for more than 24 hours, straining to urinate, repeated vomiting, difficulty breathing, or suspected toxin ingestion.
