# Whisker Works - Store Policies (v3.2, effective 1 September 2026)

Whisker Works Inc. is a retail store owned and operated by a team of techie cats:
Sir Purrcival Byte (Chief Executive Mouser), Null Pointer (CTO), Kernel Panic (Head of DevOps)
and Miss Kittens-Debug (Customer Support Lead).

## Shipping

- **Standard shipping** (5-7 business days): free for orders of **$49 or more**, otherwise **$5.99**.
- **Express shipping** ("Zoomies Delivery", 1-2 business days): **$14.99** flat rate, available in the US, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, France and Finland only.
- We ship to 27 countries. We **do not** ship live plants (cats eat them) or anything containing lilies (toxic to cats).
- Orders placed before **2 PM local warehouse time** ship the same business day.
- Every parcel is inspected by at least one cat. Minor fur deposits are a feature, not a bug.

## Returns and refunds

- Unopened items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
- Opened toys and beds can be returned within **14 days** if your cat "refuses to acknowledge the item's existence" (our official *Cat Indifference Guarantee*). Refund is issued as store credit.
- **Food and treats cannot be returned once opened**, for hygiene reasons.
- The **Premium Cardboard Box v2.0** cannot be returned once a cat has sat in it. This is a law of physics.
- Refunds are processed within **5 business days** after the return is received.
- To start a return, contact support with your order ID (format `ORD-XXXX`).

## Nine Lives Club (loyalty program)

- Free to join. Earn **1 paw point per $1** spent.
- **100 paw points = $5** store credit.
- Tiers: **Kitten** (0-499 points), **Tomcat** (500-1,499 points, free express shipping), **Top Cat** (1,500+ points, free express shipping + early access to new products + birthday treat box).
- Points expire after **9 months** of inactivity (one month per life).

## Price match

We match the price of identical products from authorised retailers within **7 days** of purchase. Marketplace sellers and "a guy at the cat show" are not eligible.

## Customer support

- Live chat with Whiskers (our AI concierge) 24/7.
- Human-staffed (well, cat-supervised) support: Monday-Friday, 8 AM - 6 PM ET at support@whiskerworks.example.
- Average first response: under 2 hours. Average purr: 25 Hz.

## Privacy

We never sell customer data. Our cats tried to sell it once for tuna; the board said no.
