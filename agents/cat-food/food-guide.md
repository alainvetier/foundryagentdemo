## Feeding basics

Source: Whisker Works Cat Care Basics, `data/knowledge/cat-care-basics.md`.

- Most adult cats do well with 2-3 measured meals a day.
- Treats should be no more than 10% of daily calories. The guide's illustrative
  200 kcal/day example permits at most 20 kcal of treats (10 WW-006 treats).
  This is an example, not a calorie prescription for an individual cat.
- Water needs include water in food. Provide fresh water and encourage drinking;
  the guide suggests placing water away from food bowls.
- Never feed onions, garlic, chocolate, grapes/raisins, alcohol or xylitol.
- For symptoms, injuries, medications or dietary management of disease, consult
  a licensed veterinarian.
- Not eating for more than 24 hours, straining to urinate, repeated vomiting,
  difficulty breathing or suspected toxin exposure warrant immediate vet help.

## General educational context

Source: general feeding guidance, not additional Whisker Works product claims.

- Look for a nutritional adequacy statement appropriate to the cat's life stage;
  do not infer "complete and balanced" from marketing phrases.
- Wet food can contribute water; dry food is convenient to portion and store.
  Neither format alone proves nutritional adequacy.
- Follow the food label and veterinary advice for portions. Weight, age,
  activity, body condition and energy density affect individual needs.
- Change foods gradually when appropriate and watch for digestive upset; consult
  a vet for persistent symptoms or special dietary needs.
- Use the manufacturer for current ingredient, storage and recall information.
  This agent has no real-time recall lookup and cannot certify a food recall-free.
